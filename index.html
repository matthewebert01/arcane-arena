Season 04 · Ranked ladder

How ranking works

Leagues

Every ranked mode has its own ladder. Climb through seven leagues; each league below Grandmaster has three tiers, from Tier 3 up to Tier 1.

Placement matches

Your first 5 ranked matches in a mode are placements. You show as Unranked while the ladder finds your level, and each result moves you further than usual (+60 / −40 RP). After the fifth match you are placed into a league.

Grandmaster

Reach 2,600 RP to enter Grandmaster, the league for the top 200 wizards on each ladder.

Skill (MMR)

The scoreboard also shows a hidden skill rating (MMR) that follows your win–loss record. Matchmaking pairs wizards of similar MMR, from Novice up to Archmage.

Ranked is coming soon. It opens once online matchmaking against real players is ready.
